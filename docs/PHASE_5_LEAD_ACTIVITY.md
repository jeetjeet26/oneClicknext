# Phase 5 — recorded internal lead notes and complete activity history

September 22, 2026. Locally verified; Phase 5 remains active.

The lead activity screen now accepts internal notes through an attributed, property-scoped operation. It no longer accepts human-supplied “email sent,” “tour completed” or other system-success labels. Confirmed system workflows retain their own activity producers and evidence. The timeline clearly labels its records and directs outcome verification to the relevant workflow.

Notes preserve their original author, creation time and content. Corrections, withdrawal and restoration require a reason and the exact displayed version; each decision commits atomically with private before/after evidence and the shared action record. Only the original author or a current manager can change a note. Earlier notes retain unknown author/time honestly; first review preserves the original record rather than inventing its initial history. Withdrawn content remains in retained history. Saved note content from an earlier property is held after a lead moves, and every read/replay rechecks current access.

Lost responses retry the same decision without duplicating notes or corrections. Recovery identity stores hashes rather than private note content in browser storage. The timeline and revision history page completely, including beyond 1,000 rows and tied/missing dates. Failed reads remain visible; delayed responses cannot cross a lead or property change. Arbitrary internal/provider metadata is no longer exposed. Repeat console inquiries retain their existing five-minute duplicate-note window through a scoped native operation. This compatibility change does not qualify the entire lead creation/edit/status lifecycle or CRM-provider note reconciliation.
