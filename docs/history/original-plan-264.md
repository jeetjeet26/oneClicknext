# Real Estate Autonomous Agency — Revised Phased Plan

**Objective:** Finish SiteForge first, finish every other product, establish dependable operation across the whole platform, then introduce agency-wide autonomy.

## Phase 0 — Contain immediate exposure

Address the identified profile-permission issue, missing-key authentication behavior, scraping protections, public-endpoint limits, and misleading simulated operational status.

Keep this phase focused on protecting current client services and subsequent development.

## Phases 1–3 — Finish SiteForge first

**Phase 1: Consistent product experience**
Unify the solo-operator journey, brand handoff, rental and for-sale workflows, and documentation. Preserve automatic scoped edits, advisory creative scoring, and one owner Launch action.

**Phase 2: Complete delivery**
Prove actual generation, WordPress preview, editing, staging, production launch, health verification, recovery, and rollback against real providers.

**Phase 3: Creative quality and release qualification**
Complete three contrasting benchmark sites, exercise representative edits, validate desktop/mobile results and conversion paths, and measure operator effort and cost.

**Exit:** SiteForge is suitable for repeated client delivery, with demonstrated recovery and rollback.

## Phase 4 — Finish the chatbot and GEO service workflows

Prioritize the products already serving clients:

- **LumaLeasing:** knowledge-grounded answers, lead capture, booking, email/calendar continuity, human handoff, integration maintenance, and failure recovery.
- **PropertyAudit:** repeatable audits, resumable execution, accurate partial/failure reporting, evidence-backed recommendations, consistent exports, and interpretable historical comparisons.

Include the TourSpark, LeadPulse, CRM, and knowledge dependencies necessary to complete these workflows.

## Phase 5 — Finish every remaining product

Each product must satisfy its intended current scope, including its interface, backend, integrations, outputs, and operational behavior.

| **Community / Property Setup** | Complete property facts, inventory, contacts, assets, brand inputs, integration setup, and readiness visibility. |
| **Shared platform surfaces** | Finish settings, supported account/team behavior, search, reports, integrations, and operational dashboards. |

Recheck SiteForge, LumaLeasing, and PropertyAudit after shared changes.

No product is considered finished merely because it has routes, screens, or passing mocked tests. Unsupported functionality must be explicitly resolved in the product scope and interface.

## Phase 6 — Mandatory whole-platform completion gate

**Agency-wide autonomy cannot begin until every product passes this gate.**

For each product:

- Its intended workflows are implemented and accessible.
- Representative end-to-end scenarios pass with actual required providers.
- Outputs and displayed statuses reflect real results.
- Retries and interruptions preserve correct state.
- Expected failures have usable recovery paths.
- Permissions, property separation, validation, and applicable usage controls are verified.
- Scheduling, monitoring, credentials, and maintenance requirements are documented and checked.
- Relevant automated regression coverage exists.
- No unresolved critical or major defect prevents dependable delivery.

Across the platform:

- Product handoffs work end to end.
- Operational dashboards use real records.
- Deployment, backups, restoration, and runtime dependencies are verified.
- Documentation matches the implementation.
- A completion register records evidence and unresolved issues for every product.

**Exit:** The entire suite is dependable for operator-led service delivery.

## Phase 7 — Introduce agency-wide autonomy

Only after Phase 6:

- Connect products through shared objectives, jobs, policies, action records, budgets, verification, recovery, and outcome measurement.
- Start with bounded GEO-to-website and leasing-knowledge cycles.
- Expand automatic execution based on demonstrated reliability and results.
- Preserve the intended owner decisions, including SiteForge’s production Launch action.

## Phase 8 — Scale

Expand across properties and service lines while tracking verified completion, operator effort, automatic recovery, cost per outcome, and client-impacting errors.

**Priority order: SiteForge → current client services → every remaining product → whole-platform qualification → agency-wide autonomy → scale.**
