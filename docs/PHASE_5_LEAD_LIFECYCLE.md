# Phase 5 — recorded lead lifecycle

September 23, 2026. Locally qualified; the holistic Phase 5 register remains open.

## Result

TourSpark now creates and edits leads through one atomic, property-scoped decision. Contact matches are shown before a separate lead can be saved with a reason; no existing person is silently overwritten. The edit form retains its original reviewed revision and keeps entered fields visible after a stale/conflicting save. Full saved contact and preference fields return to the drawer without a second status mutation.

Follow-up is an explicit selection from enabled, reviewed definitions. Creation never seeds or enables default campaigns. Changing a reported status to leased or lost stops active/paused future follow-up in the same transaction as the lead and history; reopening the lead does not restart it. A delivery already underway cannot be recalled. CRM preparation uses the existing review workflow and accurately retains setup/qualification gaps without claiming a transfer was delivered.

Current administrators/managers may decide; current members can read the scoped history. Every decision retains private input, before/after lead fields, matching-contact rationale, selected follow-up steps and actual stopping/preparation results. The six shared human actions contain identifiers/counts, exclude private contact/note content and remain training-ineligible. Reported status is not a verified commercial outcome or reward.

A stable request identity supports lost-response recovery and cancellation that fences a delayed unused request. Only the identity is stored in the browser session. Recovery reads the current lead after confirming the saved decision. Literal search, status/source summaries and paged history use the complete property scope, including more than 1,000 leads. Unrecognized historical status is visibly labeled rather than presented as new.
