# Phase 5 — recorded competitor research

Locally qualified September 16, 2026. Provider acceptance and the remaining product gates remain open.

Research now has a private saved request, one execution owner, immutable property/options/result snapshots, and atomic shared request/completion/stop records. Exact retries recover the saved result; overlapping work is held; explicit stop prevents late report saves and subsequent provider steps. Stopping a local report does not claim cancellation of an already accepted provider job. Private claims are never returned to the browser.

The API now sends the research service's required authentication header. Discovery success, unavailable/unknown response, and a queued brand-analysis job are distinguished. The job ID is retained as provider evidence. A saved-evidence mode does no provider work. A failed result retains known provider status; no automatic redispatch happens after an ambiguous reply. Immutable reports remain snapshots; queued job reconciliation and real provider acceptance belong to the remaining MarketVision/agency gates.

The report preserves competitor IDs, analysis timestamps and versions, and uses current/stale/unverified labels. The initial conservative rule requires at least three current supported records and no stale/unverified members in that sample before proposing positioning hypotheses. Current means within 30 days, at least one analyzed page, reported confidence at least 0.5, and no implausible future timestamp. This is an explicit product heuristic, not a calibrated confidence guarantee. Claims are limited to the saved sample; absent evidence never proves a market gap. Saved competitors may come from earlier searches and the displayed radius is the requested discovery radius, not verified distance for every saved record.

The console resumes saved/open research, offers explicit refresh versus a new snapshot of saved data, shows stop/recovery controls, and uses readable evidence labels. The brand brief receives a saved research ID; the server loads that property's completed snapshot. Later conversation messages retain saved market context. Legacy caller-supplied context remains a compatibility path and is not converted into verified research provenance.
