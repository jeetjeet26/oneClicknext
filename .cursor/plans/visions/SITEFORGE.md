# Vision: SiteForge

Last Updated: August 19, 2026
Document Type: Vision grounded in current implementation

## Mission

SiteForge is the autonomous web team for property websites. It commissions, generates, validates, previews, launches, and rolls back work for two equal product lanes: multifamily rental and for-sale real estate.

## Role In The Platform

SiteForge is the website execution layer of the agency. It translates an immutable brand contract, property truth, lane-specific policy, and owner intent into a living site. Brand input may be generated by BrandForge or supplied through an existing identity system; both paths must converge on the same normalized, immutable contract before creative execution begins.

## Autonomous Web Team Operating Spec

SiteForge has two equal lanes:

- **Multifamily rental:** community websites oriented around availability, floor plans, amenities, location, leasing conversion, and fair-housing-safe claims.
- **For-sale:** development, builder, brokerage, or project websites oriented around homes, plans, inventory, neighborhoods, appointments, and purchase-intent conversion.

Neither lane is the default, fallback, or adaptation of the other. They share the same quality bar and operating system while using deterministic lane-specific schemas, conversion intents, truth requirements, and policy packs.

The operating contract is:

1. **One immutable brand contract.** Generated and supplied brands normalize into the same versioned contract. The contract fixes identity-critical tokens and rules—name, marks, typography, palette, voice, positioning, and prohibited mutations—for the entire website run. The AI may execute creatively within it but may not silently rewrite identity.
2. **AI-owned workflow.** The autonomous web team owns discovery synthesis, information architecture, creative direction, copy, asset commissioning, implementation, QA, preview preparation, launch preparation, and post-launch verification. Humans provide truth, constraints, credentials, and the final launch decision; they do not project-manage intermediate agent work.
3. **Creative commissioning, not a low-level questionnaire.** Intake asks the owner for intent, references, non-negotiables, supplied assets, and business truth. The AI converts that brief into page, copy, image, motion, and interaction commissions. It must not make the owner choose implementation details, enumerate routine sections, or answer a disguised website-builder questionnaire.
4. **Owner-only preview.** The authenticated property owner/operator is the only human review surface. There is no external client-review portal, stakeholder commenting layer, or internal approval ceremony.
5. **One note-free Launch button.** Preview culminates in one `Launch` action with no required note, rationale, approval chain, or separate approve-then-publish step. Launch binds to the exact immutable previewed artifact and still must pass deterministic machine gates.
6. **Deterministic internal policy.** Compliance, accessibility, truth, quality, deployment readiness, and lane-specific constraints are evaluated internally by versioned rules and evidence—not by committee judgment. Policy outcomes are auditable even though they are not an approval ceremony.
7. **Immutable identity and fail-closed truth — scoped to facts.** SiteForge must preserve brand identity and refuse to invent property facts, inventory, pricing, availability, claims, credentials, or provider success. Missing or conflicting required *facts* block the affected output and produce a precise owner-facing request. Missing *assets* do not block: photos degrade to placeholders, rights/curation status never gates, and truth never expires on a timer.
8. **Premium quality bar, advisory enforcement.** Every launch candidate must look and behave like a bespoke **$50,000-$100,000 agency website**, not a template with generated copy. The deterministic premium-creative score measures this on every generation and accepted edit and is surfaced to the owner, but it advises rather than blocks — the solo operator decides what ships.

## Solo-Operator Doctrine

The full doctrine lives in `.cursor/rules/siteforge-solo-operator-doctrine.mdc` and is binding on all SiteForge work. In short: one owner, no approval ceremony, no rights or expiry gates, auto-executing targeted edits that never silently regenerate, an exact WordPress preview as ground truth with rendered self-verification after edits, advisory-only quality scoring, owner self-serve promotion with verified rollback, and a fixed model policy (Sonnet for targeted/structural, Fable for creative/generation, never Haiku, escalation only upward). What still fails closed: brand immutability, factual truth grounding, tenant-safe auth, schema truth, artifact identity, and rollback verification.

## Current Implementation Baseline

Materially implemented today:

- generation, analyze, status, preview, edit, deploy, rollback, list, and delete routes in `app/api/siteforge/*`
- WordPress and Cloudways deployment client in `utils/siteforge/wordpress-client.ts`
- operator preview and diagnostics UI in `components/siteforge/*`
- immutable, content-hashed blueprint revisions with exact plan and artifact
  approval identities
- deterministic ACF JSON, signed theme, and runtime-v3 package build/drift
  tooling for first-party artifacts
- default local smoke coverage for approved plan, simulated generation,
  persisted local preview architecture, and fail-closed
  quality/qualification/staging/launch/rollback gates
- an opt-in, destructive Aurora runtime-v3 lifecycle for canonical WordPress
  preview, exact-artifact qualification, Cloudways staging/promotion, immutable rollback,
  restore verification, and cleanup

Current reality:

- SiteForge is one of the deepest implemented products in the repo
- deployment, launch, and rollback implementations are materially real and
  preserve immutable artifact identities
- the default simulated lane cannot truthfully certify canonical WordPress
  preview, create Cloudways evidence, or produce a verified rollback target
- provider-backed Aurora success remains unproven until one complete opt-in run
  passes; the existence of the test is not acceptance evidence
- runtime v3 and governed extensions remain explicit opt-ins, while the
  semantic editor is enabled unless explicitly disabled
- Vertical Platform V2 is default-on for new websites; its env vars act as
  kill switches only, with no allowlist gating

## End-State Vision

SiteForge should function like a governed website operating system:

- both rental and for-sale lanes are first-class and policy-complete
- the site blueprint and brand contract are versioned, immutable for a run, and deployable
- preview shows the same truth that deploy will use
- deploys are validated before and after publish
- every deployment is reversible
- the owner sees a finished preview and one note-free Launch action
- deterministic internal gates explain failures without introducing an approval ceremony
- launch candidates consistently clear the $50,000-$100,000 bespoke-agency quality bar

Longer term, SiteForge should support bounded website optimization loops, but only once the deploy surface is fully trustworthy.

## P1 Closure Priorities

- keep edited blueprint state as the exact immutable deploy source of truth
- complete at least one real WordPress deploy and rollback validation path
- keep clean-checkout ACF/theme/runtime generation and drift checks reproducible
- reduce placeholder preview surfaces on critical content blocks
- strengthen degraded behavior when upstream brand context is weak or provider calls fail

## P2 Autonomy Contract

Before autonomy can recommend or supervise website changes, SiteForge must provide:

- explicit versioned deployable artifacts
- auditable deploy, rollback, and verification records
- policy boundaries around publish and irreversible changes
- exact binding between the owner-previewed artifact and the single Launch action
- deterministic machine-policy decisions and rollback metadata for high-risk changes
- no external client review, internal approval ceremony, required launch note, or hidden human sign-off
- consistent preview-to-deploy truth with no hidden transformation gap

## Success Metrics

- generate-to-preview completion rate
- deploy success rate
- rollback success rate
- operator time to diagnose failed deploys
- percentage of site changes that can be traced to explicit version history
